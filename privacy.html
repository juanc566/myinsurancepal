Last updated: May 26, 2026 · ACQ Lab Media LLC

Summary: We collect your information to help you find auto insurance quotes. We share it with licensed insurance partners who may contact you. You can opt out at any time.

MyInsurancePal is operated by ACQ Lab Media LLC ("Company," "we," "us," or "our"), a Wyoming limited liability company located at 1021 E Lincolnway, Suite 10325, Cheyenne, WY 82001. We operate the website myinsurancepal.net (the "Site").

2. Information We Collect

When you use our Site, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal identifiers: First name, last name, email address, phone number
  • Location data: ZIP code and state
  • Vehicle information: Number of vehicles, current insurance status, driving history
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, referring URLs
  • Consent records: Date, time, and content of your consent, captured via TrustedForm

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Match you with licensed auto insurance providers in your area
  • Share your information with insurance partners who may contact you with quotes
  • Improve our website and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Send follow-up communications about insurance options (you may opt out at any time)

4. How We Share Your Information

By submitting the form on our Site, you expressly consent to have your information shared with our network of licensed insurance partners, agents, and carriers. These third parties may contact you via phone, email, or text message regarding auto insurance quotes and offers.

We may also share your information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate our website and business
  • Analytics providers (such as Google Analytics)
  • Legal authorities when required by law

We do not sell your personal information to unrelated third parties for their own marketing purposes unrelated to insurance.

5. TCPA Consent & Communications

By submitting your information, you provide your express written consent under the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) to be contacted by MyInsurancePal and its insurance partners at the phone number provided, including via autodialed calls, pre-recorded messages, and text messages. Consent is not required to purchase any product or service.

To opt out of communications, you may reply STOP to any text message, say "remove me" during a call, or contact us at privacy@myinsurancepal.net.

6. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience and measure the performance of our advertising. This includes pixels from Meta (Facebook), Google, and our affiliate network partners. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

7. TrustedForm

We use TrustedForm by ActiveProspect to document your consent. TrustedForm records the content of the page you viewed and the actions you took when submitting your information. This certificate is retained as proof of your consent.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, typically no more than 24 months, unless required by law to retain it longer.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your state of residence, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@myinsurancepal.net.

10. California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). We do not sell personal information as defined by the CCPA. For more information or to submit a request, contact privacy@myinsurancepal.net.

11. Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a minor, please contact us immediately.
